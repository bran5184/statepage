Northeast Kansas

I have quite a biased look at Kansas as I spent the first 20 years of my life exclusively living there. Of course since I was born and raised in "Lovely Lawrence, Kansas" that's what I think of.







Lawrence

Lawrence is home to The University of Kansas. A school well known for it's basketball team that was originally coached by the inventor of Basketball. They also happen to be very good. Massachussetts Street which is our main downtown corridor. most folks shorten it to Mass Street (Fun Fact: I couldn't say the word Massachusetts until I was 16).

Topeka

Topeka is the capital of Kansas. It's also about the cheapest place to live in northeast kansas, although unless you are a government official you'll have a hard time getting hired anywhere. unless you own your own buisness (I have family in topeka)

(Broadly) Western Kansas

I consider "Western Kansas" to be anything west of Topeka and south of Wichita. So this includes everything from Manhattan, Junction City, the famous Dodge City everyone wants to get out of, and Hays.
