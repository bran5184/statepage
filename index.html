Lawrence is home to The University of Kansas. A school well known for it's basketball team that was originally coached by the inventor of Basketball. They also happen to be very good. Topeka is the capital of Kansas. It's also about the cheapest place to live in northeast kansas, although unless you are a government official you'll have a hard time getting hired anywhere. unless you own your own buisness (I have family in topeka)
